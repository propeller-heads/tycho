1.5 **Settlement** means submitting, causing, enabling, or materially facilitating a transaction on any blockchain or distributed ledger that executes all or part of a trade, swap, transfer, rebalance, liquidation, auction, intent, or economically similar operation for which the Software or a Fynd Output was used.

1.6 **Designated Router** means, for a particular network, the then-current smart contract and any required executor or auxiliary contracts identified by the Licensor in the [Router Registry](https://docs.propellerheads.xyz/tycho/for-solvers/execution/contract-addresses). 1.7 **Self-Hosted Settlement** means a Settlement generated by an instance of the Software operated by you, your Affiliate, or a contractor solely on your behalf. A Settlement obtained as the output of a separate routing or execution service supplied by the Licensor or a third party is not a Self-Hosted Settlement.

1.8 **Platform Fee** means the aggregate mandatory consideration imposed by the Licensor or its Affiliates as a condition of executing a Settlement through the Designated Router and its required executor or auxiliary contracts, regardless of recipient, label, collection location, or collection method. It excludes only Positive Slippage Capture, Revenue Share, bona fide gas and network charges, liquidity-provider and protocol fees payable to independent third parties, taxes, and fees independently chosen and retained by you. A mandatory charge for access to required settlement infrastructure is part of the Platform Fee.

1.9 **Positive Slippage Capture** means the positive difference, if any, between the actual amount of output tokens produced by a trade on-chain and the correctly quoted amount of output tokens for that trade, as simulated by the routing algorithm using the blockchain state of the block known to it when the quote was generated. Both amounts must be measured on the same basis, before Positive Slippage Capture and the Platform Fee. The reference is that correctly simulated quote, not a lower minimum-received amount or a quote recalculated using a later block. The quote and realized amount must not be artificially altered to increase or reduce Positive Slippage Capture.

1.10 **Solver Compensation** means any fee, reward, rebate, incentive, token allocation, surplus-sharing payment, auction or batch reward, quote reward, execution reward, or other consideration received by you or an Affiliate from a protocol, venue, order-flow source, customer, or other third party for or because of quoting, routing, solving, executing, or settling transactions using the Software or Fynd Outputs. Solver Compensation is included whether calculated by reference to an individual transaction, order, auction, batch, accounting period, participation, ranking, performance, or another measure, and whether paid in money, tokens, other property, or services. It excludes only a documented reimbursement solely for bona fide gas or network costs actually incurred and passed through without markup.

1.11 **Proprietary Trading Return** means a bona fide gain or loss resulting from changes in market prices or differences between market prices when you or an Affiliate buys, sells, or holds assets as principal, using its own capital and at its own market risk. It includes gains or losses from CEX-DEX and DEX-DEX arbitrage, even when the Software or Fynd Outputs are used to identify, price, route, or execute the opportunity. It excludes Solver Compensation and any separately identifiable fee, reward, incentive, rebate, commission, surplus-sharing payment, or other consideration received for providing a service or handling another person's order or transaction.

1.12 **Settlement Revenue** means all Solver Compensation and all fees, commissions, markups, service spreads, rebates, subscriptions, or other consideration received by you or an Affiliate that is specifically attributable to Settlements using the Software or Fynd Outputs, whether charged or collected on-chain, off-chain, directly, indirectly, or as part of another charge. A spread is included only where it is retained as compensation for handling third-party flow, not where it forms part of a Proprietary Trading Return. Settlement Revenue excludes trade principal, gas and network charges passed through without markup, transaction taxes collected for a public authority, refunds, and Proprietary Trading Return.

1.13 **Commercial Service** means a product or service supplied to a third party for consideration whose operation or output materially depends on the Software or Fynd Outputs, including a routing, quoting, pricing, simulation, indexing, state-streaming, data, or oracle service, or a paid supply of copies of the Software or Modifications.

1.14 **Service Revenue** means all consideration received by you or an Affiliate for a Commercial Service, excluding transaction taxes collected for a public authority, refunds, gas and network charges passed through without markup, trade principal, and amounts already counted as Settlement Revenue. If a Commercial Service is bundled with other products or services, Service Revenue is the portion reasonably attributable to the Commercial Service based on its standalone selling price or, if none exists, a reasonable and consistently applied allocation reflecting its relative value.

1.15 **Revenue Share** means twenty percent of Settlement Revenue or Service Revenue, as applicable.

## 4. Revenue share

4.1 If you or an Affiliate receives Settlement Revenue, you owe the Licensor a Revenue Share equal to twenty percent of that Settlement Revenue. Only amounts expressly collected as Revenue Share on the same revenue item are credited against this obligation. Platform Fees and Positive Slippage Capture are separate charges and do not reduce Revenue Share.

4.2 If you or an Affiliate receives Service Revenue, you owe the Licensor a Revenue Share equal to twenty percent of that Service Revenue. No Revenue Share is due merely because you use the Software internally, make it available without consideration, or earn a Proprietary Trading Return. For clarity, Solver Compensation is Settlement Revenue even when paid periodically, based on aggregate auction, batch, participation, ranking, or performance results, or denominated in a governance or reward token. It does not become a Proprietary Trading Return merely because you also deploy your own capital or bear market risk in connection with the same activity. Settlements remain subject to Section 3 in every case.

4.3 Revenue may not be omitted or reduced because it is collected by an Affiliate or other intermediary, collected off-chain, characterized as trading profit, a spread, reward, incentive, rebate, membership, subscription, data charge, support charge, or other fee, or bundled with another product or service. Classification depends on the economic substance of a receipt, not its name or accounting treatment. The same item of revenue will not be counted both as Settlement Revenue and Service Revenue.

For Revenue Share accounting, an economic receipt is counted once across you, your Affiliates, and intermediaries. A transfer of that receipt within that group does not create a second receipt.

4.4 For each calendar quarter in which you or an Affiliate receives Settlement Revenue or Service Revenue, including where all corresponding Revenue Share was collected automatically, you must within thirty days after that quarter report Settlement Revenue, Service Revenue, Revenue Shares collected automatically, and Revenue Shares remaining payable, and pay the outstanding amount under Section 4.6. Each report must identify you, the reporting period, relevant Affiliate receipts, the currency or token and value of receipts, the basis for any allocation or conversion, and the transaction references or other evidence supporting amounts already collected. Non-cash consideration is valued at its fair market value when received, using a widely recognized exchange or pricing source applied consistently or, if none exists, a reasonable documented valuation method.

4.5 You must maintain complete and accurate records supporting each report for at least three years. No more than once per calendar year, the Licensor may have an independent auditor inspect those records on at least thirty days' notice, during normal business hours and subject to reasonable confidentiality obligations. The Licensor bears the audit cost unless the audit identifies an underpayment exceeding five percent for the period audited, in which case you must reimburse the reasonable audit cost in addition to the shortfall and any interest permitted by law.

4.6 **Payment instructions.** The Licensor must publish or link reporting and payment instructions on the Router Registry page, identifying the reporting destination, payee, currency or token, network and token contract where applicable, payment destination, and payment reference.
